About Me

I am a tenured research scientist at Inria, in the Privatics team in Lyon. I study how data and AI systems can reveal sensitive information about people. My goal is to limit those risks while keeping systems useful.

My research ranges from privacy attacks on decentralized learning to anonymization of speech, mobility data, and medical images. More recently, I have been exploring privacy in agentic AI.

Privacy in collaborative learning

Inference attacks, decentralized recommendation, and confidential learning.

Anonymization & evaluation

Measuring disclosure risks in speech, mobility data, and medical imaging.

Background & education

Before joining Privatics, I worked on privacy-preserving federated learning at ENTPE (2021–2022), within the DSVD chair in partnership with Renault Group, and on private machine learning for speech processing at Inria (2019–2021), in the Magnet team.

  • PhD in Computer Science · INSA Lyon / LIRIS, 2019. Research on location privacy, re-identification attacks, and obfuscation techniques.
  • Computer Science Engineering degree · ESI, Algiers, 2016.
